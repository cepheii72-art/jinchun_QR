Text on the Top Banner

Warm spring waters
Shimmer in shades of blue.
From time to time I visit,
To keep company with the moon above the mountains.
Now and then, wind chimes echo
From far beyond the clouds.
Over the southern lake,
Willows cast their shade.
Wanwigak lies shrouded
In mist and clouds.
The wind is calm,
And the water unfurls like white silk.
