Donor Yi Jeong-hui

Born in Yongjeong-ri, Chopyeong-myeon, Jincheon-gun, Chungcheongbuk-do Province, Yi Jeong-hui was a tenth-generation descendant of Yi In-yeop of the Gyeongju Yi clan. To support the restoration of Wanwigak and the development of Chopyeong Book Village, he donated some 800 old books that had been preserved by his family. Some of the books in the original collection were destroyed in a fire in the late Joseon period. The surviving volumes continued to be preserved in the family’s sarangchae, the traditional men’s quarters of the house, and were kept even after the family moved to Seoul. At one point, the collection was also entrusted to the Jangseogak Archives of the Academy of Korean Studies. The donated books will be permanently preserved by Jincheon County and continue to serve as valuable resources for exhibitions and academic research.
